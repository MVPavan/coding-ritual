# Optional exploration

Use when multiple directions are live. Name the user outcome, constraints and
riskiest assumptions before generating alternatives. Include reuse or doing
nothing where they plausibly satisfy the need.

Generate only enough distinct options to expose the tradeoff. Compare them on
user value, feasibility, evidence, cost and reversibility; distinguish facts from
bets. Test the assumption most likely to reverse the choice before elaborating
low-value details. Keep rejected options and the reason for rejection when they
will matter to a later decision.

Converge on a recommended direction and a checkable next step. A saved one-pager
is optional; an explicit request for in-chat ideas should stay in chat. Optional
lenses and examples remain at `frameworks.md`,
`refinement-criteria.md` and `examples.md` in this folder;
use only the relevant lens.
