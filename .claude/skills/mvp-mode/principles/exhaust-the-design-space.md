# Exhaust the design space

The first workable idea for something new is one point among the answers,
not the answer.

- Before settling the decision, lay out the materially different ways to
  meet it: different mechanisms, not variations of one.
- Say what each costs and what it gives up, and choose with stated reasons,
  including why the others lost.
- Weighing them in your reasoning or reply is enough; no artifact is
  required. Build running versions to compare only when the decision is an
  interaction, an architecture or behaviour judged by feel, which is
  `prototype`'s case.
