# perspective-council

Use only when the user explicitly requests a perspective council or war room. Answer ordinary decisions and critique requests without a council.

**Boundary:** Runs an explicitly requested council of perspectives or roles (a war room); a council of named models with a judge is model-council.

<!-- registry:generated traits -->
- `declared-scale`: Choose and state the council's scale before dispatch, naming membership and whether peer review is included.
- `opposing-lens-set`: Draw advisors from a fixed set of opposing lenses, each contributing a defined kind of challenge.
- `neutral-framing`: Frame one neutral shared brief for every member, stating the question, constraints and uncertainty with no preferred conclusion.
- `member-isolation`: Give each member the shared brief in isolation so none sees another's response; sequential dispatch is allowed.
- `evidence-calibration`: Require each role to give evidence and calibrated uncertainty rather than forced certainty or ignored risk.
- `anonymized-peer-review`: When peer review is included, anonymize responses before reviewers compare their reasoning and gaps.
- `independent-synthesis`: A fresh synthesizer that did not participate receives every member's full report and produces agreement, disputes, blind spots and a best-supported recommendation, treating agreement as no proof. (The synthesizer is a fresh chair who restores attribution after anonymized peer review and ends with a practical next step.)
- `no-silent-substitution`: Never backfill a missing member with your own answer or a silent substitute; report the gap and whether the synthesizer saw an incomplete set.
- `transcript-on-request`: Save a council transcript only when it is requested.
