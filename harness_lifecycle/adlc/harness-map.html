01The lifecycle itself

Eight stages, left to right. Building, Verification and Integration repeat once per increment. Anything found in use, such as an incident or a scan finding, re-enters at Intake as a new request. The crosscutting concerns run under every stage.

03How the flow is established

The same lifecycle, but each harness moves work from step to step differently. The difference is who causes the next step.

step the model moves it step you type it gate you decide step unavailable without the engine

Ours, skills only: model-chained, with gates at the artifacts

You ask in plain words. The model picks each skill from its description. Work stops at each artifact (spec, structure, commit) until you approve it. Beads plus repo docs hold the state. Without the engine, there is no unattended walk: /run-phases and /phase-execution both call the contractor.

  1. YOUask in plain words
  2. MODELbrainstorming → spec
  3. GATEapprove spec
  4. MODELplanning: decompose
  5. GATEapprove structure
  6. MODELplanning: elaborate + critic
  7. ENGINE ONLY/run-phases walk
  8. MODELexecution task loop: implementer → checks → review (≤5)
  9. GATEauthorize commit
  10. MODELbeads close + resume note

Pocock: a chain of your keystrokes

The main-flow skills are user-only, and no skill can invoke a user-only skill, so you type every hop. The issue tracker holds the state (spec issue, tickets, labels). Work runs without you only inside /implement-spec. Upstream's stated position: truly unattended work belongs in an external deterministic loop.

  1. YOU/grill-with-docs
  2. GATEshared understanding
  3. YOU/to-spec
  4. GATEconfirm seams
  5. YOU/to-tickets
  6. GATEapprove breakdown
  7. YOU/implement or /implement-spec
  8. MODELtdd → code-review → commit
  9. MODELpr body
  10. YOUmerge (no skill)
  11. YOU/retro

pstack: one router mode, the playbook drives

You type /poteto-mode once, optionally with a grant such as "run until done". The router copies the matching playbook's steps into the todo list and the model walks them. GitHub PRs and CI are the state. You come back for "go" on a plan, for merge (unless the grant covers it), and for the always-pause list. A /loop wake keeps long runs alive.

  1. YOU/poteto-mode task (+grant)
  2. MODELmatch playbook → todo
  3. MODELhow / why
  4. MODELarchitect + arena
  5. MODELdelegate in a worktree
  6. MODELverify on the real surface
  7. MODELinterrogate (if contested)
  8. MODELopening-a-pr
  9. GATE"babysit" / "ship", or the grant covers it
  10. MODELshipping: verified run lands
  11. MODELreflect / correct

What the workflow engine adds back for us (left out above)

The engine supplies:

  • The unattended phase walk (/run-phases).
  • A deterministic inner loop: implement → debrief → review.
  • Host verification after every node.
  • A signed human ship gate bound to the exact commit.
  • Budgets: 26 activations and 3 region entries.
  • The run ledger, the recovery tree, and a monitor.

With the engine, Integration moves from None to Strong, and Orchestration and Governance move to Strong. Two things still block full autonomy: the ship gate is signed per stage, and the monitor's re-wake hook is not wired.

05What this tells us

Everyone stops at merge

No harness covers Validation, Monitoring or Response. The only Release skill is ours, and it ships only our own plugin. For all three, the lifecycle effectively ends at Integration.

We are strongest at "agree" and "enforce"

Clarification, the Review critic contract, and Governance hooks are our edge. Hooks make us the only one of the three that enforces anything in code.

Without the engine, we can't land

Integration is None and every hop needs your approval. pstack reaches autonomy through a grant, a verdict from a non-author, and a /loop wake; it needs no engine.

pstack covers the most, all in prose

It is strongest at Grounding, Testing on the real surface, Integration, and Evaluation. It is weak at Clarification by design, has no Discovery skill, and depends on GitHub.

Borrow candidates, by sub-stage

Grounding: how/why. Testing: a real-surface verify skill. Integration: verified-run landing. Retrospective and Codification: reflect/correct. Evaluation: the eval playbook. Triage: grant parsing. Each one still goes through harness-evaluate and the ledger.
